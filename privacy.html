Privacy policy

education-video-sketches is a personal tool used only by its owner, Marian Gheorghe, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel (@gmarian-edu).

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses two Google permissions: upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload), and read the channel's basic details to confirm the right channel (youtube.readonly).

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner. No other personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service.
